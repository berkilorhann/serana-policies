# Privacy Policy — Emai

**Effective date:** September 12, 2026  
**Developer:** CosmicLoopByte  
**Contact:** cosmicloopbyte@gmail.com  
**App:** Emai — AI Companion

This Privacy Policy explains what information Emai ("the App", "we", "us")
collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Please read this policy
before using the App.

## 1. Summary

- Emai does **not** require a traditional account, email address, or phone
  number.
- Your identity in the App is an **anonymous, randomly generated App user ID**
  created on first launch.
- We do **not** use third-party advertising SDKs, track you across other
  companies' apps or websites, or sell your personal information.
- Chat messages and recent conversation context are processed by our servers
  and AI service providers to generate companion replies.
- If you start a voice call, microphone audio is streamed to our servers and
  voice AI providers to provide the call.
- Payments are processed by **Apple through the App Store**. We never receive
  or store your payment-card details.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information you provide

| Data | When | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Display name | Profile setup or profile editing | Personalizing conversations and your profile |
| Chat messages and recent conversation context | While chatting | Generating AI companion replies and maintaining the conversation experience |
| Voice audio | During a voice call, after you grant microphone permission | Transcribing your speech, generating AI responses, and providing synthesized voice replies |
| Custom character details, such as a character name, bio, traits, and personality prompt | When you create a custom character | Creating, storing, and displaying your custom AI companion |
| Feedback information, including an optional email address, subject, and message | When you contact support through the App | Responding to support requests and improving the service |
| Reports and related messages | When you submit a report | Reviewing safety, abuse, and content issues |
| In-app actions, such as character matches, gifts sent, media unlocked, and relationship progress | While using the App | Operating personalization, matching, gifting, media, and relationship features |

### 2.2 Information collected automatically

| Data | When | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Anonymous App user ID (a random UUID generated by the App) | First launch | Identifying your App profile to our backend and RevenueCat |
| Platform, app version, device language, country or region setting, and basic request metadata | When the App connects to our services | Localization, security, debugging, and service operation |
| Purchase history and entitlement information, such as subscription status and credit balance | During purchases, restoration, and App use | Validating purchases and providing paid features |
| Product interaction and service activity, such as matched characters and feature usage required to maintain your App state | While using the App | Providing App functionality, preventing abuse, and maintaining your experience |

Our hosting and content-delivery providers may also process network information,
such as an IP address, as necessary to establish a connection, protect the
service, and deliver content.

### 2.3 Information we do NOT collect

- A mandatory real name, email address, or phone number
- Payment-card or bank-account details
- Precise GPS location
- Address-book contacts
- Photos or videos from your personal photo library
- Camera data
- Advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking

Microphone audio is processed only when you actively start a voice call and
grant microphone permission.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

1. Provide and operate the App, including conversations, voice calls,
   characters, matching, gifts, media, and relationship features
2. Generate AI responses and synthesized voice replies
3. Personalize the experience and maintain limited conversation memories and
   relationship state
4. Process and honor purchases, subscriptions, restorations, refunds, and
   credit balances
5. Respond to support requests and content reports
6. Maintain security, prevent abuse and fraud, troubleshoot errors, and improve
   reliability
7. Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

We do **not** use your information for third-party advertising, and we do
**not** sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral
advertising.

## 4. AI-Generated Content and Chat Processing

- Messages you send and recent conversation context are transmitted to our
  backend hosted on Google Firebase / Google Cloud and forwarded to an AI
  service provider to generate the companion's reply.
- The complete chat display history is primarily stored locally on your device.
  Limited derived memories, relationship state, custom-character information,
  and operational records may be stored on our servers and linked to your
  anonymous App user ID.
- During voice calls, microphone audio and conversation context are processed
  in real time by our backend and voice AI providers. We do not intentionally
  retain raw microphone recordings after they are no longer needed to provide
  the call, although providers may temporarily process technical data under
  their own retention and security practices.
- **Do not share sensitive personal information in chats or calls**, such as
  government identification numbers, financial details, health records, or
  precise location. Messages and calls are processed as conversation data.
- AI responses are machine-generated fiction. They are not written by a human
  and do not constitute medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice.

## 5. Payments and Subscriptions

- All purchases, including subscriptions and credit packs, are processed by
  **Apple through the App Store**. We never receive or store your payment-card
  details.
- We use **RevenueCat** as a subscription and entitlement-management service.
  RevenueCat receives your anonymous App user ID, purchase and transaction
  information, and subscription status to validate purchases, prevent fraud,
  provide entitlements, and produce subscription analytics. See the
  [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).
- Refunds, cancellations, and billing disputes are handled under Apple's App
  Store policies. You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple
  Account subscription settings.

## 6. Service Providers (Data Processors)

We disclose information only to service providers needed to operate the App or
when required by law:

| Provider | Purpose | Data involved |
|---|---|---|
| Google Firebase / Google Cloud | Backend hosting, database, API operation, AI and voice services | App user ID, profile fields, chat context, voice-call data, custom-character data, in-app actions, and technical metadata |
| xAI or another configured AI language-model provider | Generating text replies and conversation features | Chat messages, recent conversation context, character instructions, and limited associated metadata |
| ElevenLabs and/or Google Cloud voice services | Speech and synthesized voice features | Voice audio, reply text, voice configuration, and limited technical metadata |
| RevenueCat | Purchase validation, entitlement management, subscription analytics, and fraud prevention | Anonymous App user ID, purchase history, transaction identifiers, and subscription status |

| Cloudflare and other content-delivery providers | Hosting and delivering App media and other static content | IP address, requested content, and technical request metadata |

Service providers process information under their own terms and privacy
notices and are permitted to use it only as allowed by their agreements and
applicable law.

## 7. Data Storage and Security

- Server-side information is primarily stored on Google Cloud infrastructure.
  Data in transit is protected using TLS encryption.
- On your device, the App stores your anonymous App user ID, preferences,
  conversation history, relationship state, purchase status, and cached content
  using iOS storage mechanisms.
- Access to production systems is restricted to authorized personnel and
  service providers who need access to operate, secure, and support the App.
- No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot
  guarantee absolute security, but we use commercially reasonable technical and
  organizational safeguards.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

- We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the
  App, maintain security and fraud-prevention records, resolve disputes, and
  comply with legal obligations.
- Local chat history and preferences remain on your device until you clear them,
  delete your App data, or uninstall the App.
- You can use **Settings > Delete Account** in the App to delete the user profile
  and associated server-side data linked to your App user ID and reset locally
  stored App data.
- Certain minimal transaction, fraud-prevention, deletion, security, backup, or
  legal-compliance records may be retained when reasonably necessary. These
  records are not used to restore deleted conversation or profile content.
- You may also request deletion or assistance by emailing
  **cosmicloopbyte@gmail.com**. Because the App does not use a traditional
  account, we may ask you to demonstrate control of the relevant App user ID.
  We aim to respond to verified requests within 30 days or within the period
  required by applicable law.

Uninstalling the App removes locally stored App data but may not, by itself,
delete information already stored on our servers. Use the in-app Delete Account
feature before uninstalling if you want to request deletion of linked
server-side data.

## 9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR/UK GDPR, the California
Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, and Türkiye's KVKK, you may have
rights to:

- Access the personal information we hold about you
- Request correction or deletion
- Object to or restrict certain processing
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
- Receive a portable copy of eligible information
- Appeal or complain to your local data-protection authority

To exercise a privacy right, contact **cosmicloopbyte@gmail.com** or use the
in-app deletion feature. Because we do not require a traditional account, we may
need to verify control of the App user ID connected to the request.

we learn that a minor has provided personal information, we will take reasonable
steps to delete it. Parents or guardians who believe a minor has used the App
should contact us.
